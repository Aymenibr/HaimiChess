## Privacy Policy for Chess Game Submitter This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information or sensitive user data. - No account or email login is required. - The extension only accesses public Chess.com game data via their official API. - The only data stored is your Chess.com username, locally in your browser using `localStorage`, for convenience. - No user data is shared with third parties or used for tracking, profiling, or advertising purposes. For questions or concerns, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support page.
